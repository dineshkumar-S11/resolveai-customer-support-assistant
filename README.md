# 🤖 ResolveAI – Customer Support Resolution Assistant

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Customer support teams handle a large volume of customer queries and support tickets daily. Manual ticket analysis and resolution can be time-consuming, leading to delays in response times and reduced customer satisfaction.

The objective of this project is to develop an intelligent Customer Support Resolution Assistant that helps support teams quickly identify issues, track ticket status, and provide AI-assisted resolution recommendations for faster and more efficient customer service.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

ResolveAI is an AI-powered customer support dashboard designed to streamline ticket management and improve operational efficiency.

The platform enables support teams to:

- Manage customer support tickets
- Track escalated and resolved issues
- Visualize support analytics
- Receive AI-generated resolution suggestions
- Improve response time and customer satisfaction
